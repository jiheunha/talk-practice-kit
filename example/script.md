# Example talk: a small weather station on the roof

## Slide 1. A weather station on the roof (0:20)

*Smile and wait for the room to settle.*

Good afternoon, everyone. Last spring our lab put a small weather station on the roof of the building. Today I will show you what we measured and what surprised us.

## Slide 2. What we measured (0:30)

The station records temperature, wind speed and rainfall every ten minutes. Over six months it collected about twenty-six thousand readings.

The roof was warmer than the official KAIST station on most afternoons. The gap was largest on calm, sunny days. Dr. Kim thinks the dark roof surface explains most of it.

## Slide 3. What comes next (0:20)

*Point at the map.*

Next year we want to add two more stations in Daejeon, one near the river and one on the hill. Thank you for listening. I am happy to take questions.
